About Me

In essence, I’m a builder who is obsessed with the "eureka" moment—that feeling when complex, scattered pieces of information finally click into place. Whether I’m working on software or mapping out an intricate build in Path of Exile, I love the process of visualizing a solution and making it work. When I’m not deep in the code, I’m usually at the climbing gym working on a bouldering problem, prepping meals for the week, or testing my reflexes in competitive FPS and fighting games. I’m also a massive fan of metalcore. It’s all about finding that balance between tackling big professional challenges and keeping my mind sharp through the things I love.
