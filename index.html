A housing price prediction model trained with XGBoost paired with a simple frontend to allow for user interaction. The RoofUS model was trained on aggregated data from multiple real estate sites with enhancement sets like hospital proximity, air quality, school district, etc. Achieved within 5% accurate predictions within year range of the training set.
